# Commercial use note

## Where this project stands now
This is personal coursework. I don't charge for it, no business uses it,
and it runs on a sample of public Wake County parcel records.

## What would make it commercial
- **Charging money**: selling access, subscriptions, or reports built from it.
- **Using it inside a business**: for example, a real estate firm using it
  to screen deals, even if no one pays for the tool itself.
- **Offering it to clients or the public as a service.**

## What each provider's terms would require
| Provider | Today | If commercial |
|---|---|---|
| Claude (Anthropic) | Claude.ai consumer terms, for individuals | Move to the API under Anthropic's Commercial Terms, or a Team/Enterprise plan. Can't resell Claude or build a competing AI product. |
| Render (host) | Free instance, meant for hobby/testing | Paid instance. Render says free instances aren't for production; it sleeps after 15 min of no traffic. Can't resell Render's service. |
| Wake County (data) | Public open data, Creative Commons Attribution | CC BY generally allows commercial use with credit to Wake County. Data has no accuracy guarantee, so a business product needs a disclaimer and should check the county's own terms of use. |
| Supabase (database) | Free plan | Likely a paid plan for uptime and backups. |

## Before going commercial I would
1. Read each provider's full current terms (these change).
2. Add attribution to Wake County on the page.
3. Add a disclaimer that records aren't official and may be out of date.
4. Move off free tiers.
